About CHEM & GO

Our Mission

CHEM & GO is designed to make learning chemistry engaging and accessible. By exploring chemical reactions in our daily lives, we aim to help students discover, understand, and identify chemistry everywhere.

Development

This application was developed as a project to demonstrate gamified learning in a mobile-first environment. It utilizes HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to create an interactive experience.
